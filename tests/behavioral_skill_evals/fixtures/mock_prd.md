# Smart Contribution Nudges PRD

## Problem

Retirement investors often leave employer-match dollars unclaimed because the
contribution-setting flow does not explain the match threshold at the moment of
choice.

## Goals

- Increase the share of eligible users selecting at least the match threshold.
- Avoid advice-like language and preserve user control.

## Success Metrics

- Primary: match-threshold adoption rate within 14 days.
- Guardrails: complaint rate, opt-out rate, contribution reversals.
